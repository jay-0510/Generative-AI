Company Handbook: Policies and Procedures

Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to manager
approval. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are
reviewed every six months. Remote employees must be reachable during core
hours, 10 AM to 4 PM local time.

Vacation Policy
Full-time employees accrue 15 vacation days per year during their first
three years, increasing to 20 days after three years of service. Vacation
requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR
portal. Unused vacation days roll over up to a maximum of 5 days into the
following year.

Onboarding Process
New hires complete a two-week onboarding program covering company systems,
security training, and team introductions. Each new employee is assigned a
mentor from their department for their first 90 days. IT equipment is
provisioned before the employee's start date.

Expense Reimbursement Policy
Business expenses under $75 do not require pre-approval but must be
submitted with a receipt within 30 days. Expenses over $75 require manager
pre-approval. Travel expenses (flights, hotels) should be booked through
the corporate travel portal whenever possible.

Equipment and IT Policy
Employees are issued a laptop and standard peripherals upon hire.
Additional equipment requests (monitors, ergonomic accessories) go through
the IT request portal and are typically fulfilled within 5 business days.
Lost or damaged equipment should be reported to IT immediately.

Performance Review Cycle
Formal performance reviews occur twice a year, in June and December.
Reviews include self-assessment, manager feedback, and goal-setting for
the following period. Compensation adjustments, when applicable, are
communicated within 30 days of the review cycle closing.

Parental Leave Policy
Eligible employees receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the
birth, adoption, or fostering of a child. Leave can be taken continuously
or split into two blocks within the first year, subject to manager
coordination.

Code of Conduct Overview
Employees are expected to act with honesty and professionalism, respect
confidential company and customer information, and report any conflicts
of interest to HR. Violations are reviewed by HR and may result in
disciplinary action up to and including termination.
