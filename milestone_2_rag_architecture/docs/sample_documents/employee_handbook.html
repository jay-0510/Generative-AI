Northstar Employee Handbook

Version 1.0 | Effective date: January 15, 2026 | Owner: People Operations

Purpose

This handbook explains Northstar's workplace expectations, employee benefits, and approval processes. It applies to all full-time and part-time employees.

Working Hours and Attendance

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time. Employees should notify their manager before 9:30 AM if illness or an emergency prevents them from working. Repeated unreported absences may result in a performance review.

Leave Requests

Submit planned leave requests in the HR portal at least ten business days before the first day of leave. The employee's direct manager approves requests of five business days or fewer. People Operations approves requests longer than five business days.

Equipment and Security

Employees must use company-managed laptops for company work and enable multi-factor authentication. Report a lost device to the Security Operations team within one hour. Security Operations may remotely disable a missing device.

Expenses

Employees must attach itemized receipts to expense reports. Expenses above $500 require written manager approval before purchase. Finance reviews approved reports every Friday.

Questions about this handbook should be sent to People Operations.
