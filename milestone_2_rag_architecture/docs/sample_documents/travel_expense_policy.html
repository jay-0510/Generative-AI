Northstar Travel and Expense Policy

Version 2.1 | Effective date: February 1, 2026 | Owner: Finance

Purpose

This policy defines reimbursable business travel and expense requirements. Its goal is to control costs while enabling necessary customer, training, and team travel.

Before You Travel

Submit a travel request in the Travel Portal before booking. A department manager must approve estimated trip costs above $1,000. International travel also requires Security Operations approval at least fifteen business days before departure.

Booking Rules

Book economy airfare for flights shorter than six hours. Use the preferred booking tool whenever it offers a reasonable option. Hotel reimbursement is limited to $250 per night before taxes unless Finance grants a written exception.

Expense Submission

Submit an itemized expense report within ten calendar days after returning from a trip. Attach receipts for every single expense of $25 or more. Finance rejects reports without the required receipts or approvals.

Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Personal entertainment, minibar purchases, parking fines, and upgrades for personal preference are not reimbursable. If a flight is cancelled, contact the Travel Desk before buying a replacement ticket that costs more than $750.

The Finance Travel Desk can answer booking and reimbursement questions.
